# Phase 6.4
Growl applies -1 to outgoing attack rolls; Leer and Tail Whip each apply +1 to incoming opposing attacks per failed Wisdom save, capped at five stacks per move. Effects expire after ten rounds and clear on recall. Move DC uses existing move power selection. Dodge consumes an action and imposes disadvantage on incoming attacks until the next turn (prototype assumes visibility). Manual resolution spends a standard action and PP, applies DM-entered final damage and logs a reason; other effects must be adjudicated outside the automated state. Manual resolution deliberately trusts the DM for range and unsupported mechanics. Full app compilation/browser testing required locally.
