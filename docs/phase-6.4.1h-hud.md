# Single-screen battle HUD
Desktop encounters use a viewport-fixed HUD with initiative, independent roster panels, a zoomable battlefield, compact action cards and tabbed history. Settings and browser fullscreen are optional. Setup and short/narrow viewports retain normal scrolling. Large maps and long rosters scroll within their panels. Move details expand independently of move availability. No engine changes.

Browser checks: start sandbox and roster encounters, switch action/history tabs, zoom and pan the grid, resolve opportunity attacks and physical dice, toggle fullscreen and settings, finish and reset. Verify at 1920×1080 and 1366×768. Engine regression tests do not validate browser layout.
