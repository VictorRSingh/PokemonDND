# Phase 6.3.2 — Tactical movement and reactions
Grid movement displays remaining feet, base speed and adjacent reachable squares. Dash adds walking speed and spends the action; Disengage spends the action and suppresses opportunity attacks for the turn. Leaving reach prompts the DM to choose eligible reaction moves or decline, or cancel the step. Reaction execution is currently limited to Pokémon against Pokémon with digital rolls in the UI; manual rolls and trainer attacks remain outside the prototype. Square cells display sprites using the existing remote sprite source. Movement is still one step at a time. No database changes.
