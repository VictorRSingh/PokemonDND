# Trainer prototype
Campaign character tables currently contain names and roster links only. Group roster setup supplies shared encounter-local HP, AC and potion count; level 1, ability scores 10 and walking 30 remain defaults. No database writes or migrations. Select the trainer in the acting combatant dropdown when its initiative slot is active. Unarmed attack uses STR plus proficiency, 1 + STR damage; potion restores up to 10 HP and consumes one local item; Help grants advantage on the next automated attack. All cost an action and require a target within 5 ft on grid. Help is a simplified prototype support effect, not full tabletop Help timing. Trainer saving throws, persistent inventory, trainer targets and full character sheets are deferred. Existing shared initiative rules apply.
