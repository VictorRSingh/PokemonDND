# Passive abilities (5.11.2)
Eleven handlers cover status immunity, Levitate, Thick Fat and Heatproof. Damage modifiers apply after type effectiveness; status application helpers accept encounter context to honor campaign toggles. Existing context-free calls retain their prior behavior. Unsupported abilities still require manual attack resolution. Ground and ice attacks are not yet automated; their hooks are available for future moves. Triggered abilities and other passive effects remain future work. For the playable prototype, select supported abilities or disable the ability rule.
