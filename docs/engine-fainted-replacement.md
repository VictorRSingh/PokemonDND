# Phase 5.10.1 — Fainted replacement

`faintedReplacementOptions(state, outgoingId)` lists healthy recalled reserves of the same trainer and side, excluding existing initiative members. In trainer-participating grid battles recall requires a trainer within 60 feet. `switchBattlePokemon` accepts an optional destination for fainted grid replacements; release must be within 15 feet of the trainer and unoccupied. Arena and trainer-free grid behavior retain the previous placement convention. Turn advancement blocks while a recall-eligible fainted Pokémon has reserves. A replacement inherits the slot, preserves spent action resources, and has no movement or reaction until its next turn (existing conservative policy). Out-of-range recall can be deferred. Voluntary switching range and release placement, incoming status initialization, and fully occupied release areas remain future work. Baseline reconstructed from uploaded patches through 5.9.6.
